Title: Essential Financial Terms and Concepts

1. Budget:
A financial statement expressing planned expenditures, revenues, and savings allocations across a defined timeframe.

2. Cash Flow:
The net balance of money flowing into (inflows) and out of (outflows) personal accounts over a measured duration. Positive cash flow occurs when income exceeds expenditures.

3. Discretionary Spending:
Purchases for non-essential goods and services that a consumer wants rather than needs for fundamental survival (e.g., luxury items, entertainment, leisure).

4. Fixed Expenses:
Recurring expenses that do not vary in cost from month to month (e.g., rent, fixed mortgage payment, auto loan payment).

5. Variable Expenses:
Expenses that fluctuate depending on consumption habits, prices, or seasonal factors (e.g., utility bills, groceries, leisure dining).

6. Sinking Fund:
A strategic reserve created by setting aside incremental sums of cash periodically for a predetermined future expense (e.g., holiday gifts, property taxes, auto repairs).

7. Net Worth:
The total value of all assets (cash, investments, real estate) minus all liabilities and outstanding debts (loans, mortgages, credit balances).

8. Compound Interest:
Interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from prior compounding periods, enabling exponential asset growth over extended time horizons.
